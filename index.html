NURSING PROGRAMMES

Nursing Courses in Bangalore

The pivotal role of nurses in the healthcare system has consistently driven the need for high-quality nursing education. This growing demand for academic excellence and professionally trained healthcare practitioners has led to the development of a wide range of nursing courses in Bangalore. These programmes are offered across diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate levels to cater to students at different stages of their academic journey.

Each of these nursing courses is carefully structured to strengthen students’ theoretical understanding, clinical knowledge, and practical competencies. With a strong emphasis on skill development and professional training, these programmes equip aspiring nurses with the expertise required to excel in the evolving field of healthcare. Let us explore the various nursing courses offered by some of the top nursing colleges in Bangalore, along with their eligibility criteria and course duration, to help you choose the programme that best aligns with your career aspirations.

General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM)

3 Years Duration

Eligibility: Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent with a minimum aggregate of 40%–50% from a recognised board.

The GNM nursing course is a diploma-level nursing programme designed to prepare students for a rewarding career in patient care and healthcare services. The course focuses on building a strong foundation in nursing principles, clinical practices, and midwifery care, enabling students to deliver quality healthcare across diverse medical settings.

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc) in Nursing

Eligibility: Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent with a minimum aggregate of 45%–50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) from a recognised board.

The B.Sc Nursing course is designed to equip students with comprehensive knowledge and practical expertise in nursing and healthcare. The course focuses on building a strong foundation in nursing sciences, patient care, community health, and clinical practices through a combination of academic learning and hands-on clinical training. Also, the B.Sc nursing course duration is 4 years.

Post Basic B.Sc Nursing (P.B.B.Sc Nursing)

Eligibility: Candidates must have completed General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) and be registered as a Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife (RNRM) with the State Nursing Council.

The Post Basic B.Sc Nursing (P.B.B.Sc Nursing) is an undergraduate bridge programme designed for registered nurses seeking to advance their academic qualifications and professional expertise in nursing. The course focuses on enhancing clinical knowledge, leadership abilities, research aptitude, and specialized nursing competencies through advanced theoretical learning and practical training.

Master of Science (M.Sc) in Nursing

The Master of Science (M.Sc) in Nursing is a postgraduate programme designed to provide advanced theoretical knowledge, clinical expertise, and research-based competencies in specialized areas of nursing. The M.Sc Nursing course focuses on strengthening leadership, critical thinking, clinical decision-making, and advanced patient care skills through in-depth academic learning and practical training especially with the various specializations that are offered.

Eligibility: Candidates must have completed B.Sc Nursing / P.B.B.Sc Nursing with a minimum aggregate of 55% from a recognised institution and must be registered as a Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife (RNRM) with the State Nursing Council.

Admissions to nursing courses generally vary based on the programme chosen. While some courses offer admission based on academic merit, others require candidates to qualify through state-level or university/college-specific entrance examinations.

Everything you need to know about nursing admissions in Bangalore.

Students can pursue various nursing programmes based on their educational qualifications and career goals, including GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery), B.Sc Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc Nursing, and M.Sc Nursing. The eligibility requirements vary by course. For B.Sc Nursing and GNM, candidates are generally required to have completed Class 12 from a recognized board. Post Basic B.Sc Nursing is intended for registered nursing professionals while M.Sc Nursing requires a bachelor's degree in nursing from a recognised institution.
The admission process differs depending on the course and institution. While some nursing programmes offer merit-based admissions, others require candidates to qualify in state-level, university-level or institution-specific entrance examinations.
Nursing graduates can pursue careers in hospitals, clinics, community healthcare centres, educational institutions, research organisations, and public health departments. They can also specialise in areas such as critical care, paediatric nursing, psychiatric nursing, community health nursing, and medical-surgical nursing.
