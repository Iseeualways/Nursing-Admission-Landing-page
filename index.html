Nursing Courses in Bangalore

The pivotal role of nurses in the healthcare system has consistently driven the need for high-quality nursing education. This growing demand for academic excellence and professionally trained healthcare practitioners has led to the development of a wide range of nursing courses in Bangalore. These programmes are offered across diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate levels to cater to students at different stages of their academic journey.
Each of these nursing courses is carefully structured to strengthen students' theoretical understanding, clinical knowledge, and practical competencies. With a strong emphasis on skill development and professional training, these programmes equip aspiring nurses with the expertise required to excel in the evolving field of healthcare.
Let us explore the various nursing courses offered by some of the top nursing colleges in Bangalore, along with their eligibility criteria and course duration, to help you choose the programme that best aligns with your career aspirations.

Diploma Program
General Nursing & Midwifery(GNM)
Duration — 3 Years
Eligibility: Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent with a minimum aggregate of 40%–50% from a recognised board. This programme is a diploma-level nursing course designed to prepare students for a rewarding career in patient care and healthcare services. The course focuses on building a strong foundation in nursing principles, clinical practices, and midwifery care, enabling students to deliver quality healthcare across diverse medical settings.
Apply for GNM
Undergraduate
Bachelor of Science in Nursing
Duration — 4 Years
Eligibility: Candidates must have completed 10+2 or equivalent with a minimum aggregate of 45%–50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (PCB) from a recognised board. This is an undergraduate programme designed to equip students with comprehensive knowledge and practical expertise in nursing and healthcare. The course focuses on building a strong foundation in nursing sciences, patient care, community health, and clinical practices through a combination of academic learning and hands-on clinical training.
Apply for B.Sc
Post Basic
Post Basic B.Sc Nursing
Duration — 2 Years
Eligibility: Candidates must have completed General Nursing and Midwifery (GNM) and be registered as a Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife (RNRM) with the State Nursing Council. The Post Basic B.Sc Nursing (P.B.B.Sc Nursing) is an undergraduate bridge programme designed for registered nurses seeking to advance their academic qualifications and professional expertise in nursing. The course focuses on enhancing clinical knowledge, leadership abilities, research aptitude, and specialized nursing competencies through advanced theoretical learning and practical training.

Advanced Specialisations

Postgraduate Nursing Courses

M.Sc Nursing specialisations offer concentrated expertise in specific clinical domains. All specialisations run for 2 years, preparing nurses for leadership and advanced practice roles.

Eligibility Note
B.Sc Nursing / P.B.B.Sc Nursing with minimum 45–55% aggregate and one year of clinical or teaching experience.
M.Sc in Medical-Surgical Nursing

Duration: 2 Years
Eligibility: Candidates must have completed B.Sc Nursing / P.B.B.Sc Nursing with a minimum aggregate of 55% from a recognised institution and must be registered as a Registered Nurse and Registered Midwife (RNRM) with the State Nursing Council.
The Master of Science (M.Sc) in Nursing is a postgraduate programme designed to provide advanced theoretical knowledge, clinical expertise, and research-based competencies in specialised areas of nursing. The course focuses on strengthening leadership, critical thinking, clinical decision-making, and advanced patient care skills through in-depth academic learning and practical training, preparing students for senior roles in healthcare, nursing education, administration, and specialised clinical practice. Students can pursue specializations that include Obstetrics and Gynaecological Nursing, Paediatric Nursing, Community Health Nursing, Cardiothoracic Nursing etc. thereby enabling them to build expertise in specific domains of modern healthcare.
